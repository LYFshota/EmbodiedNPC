## Project modifications

The Unity café adaptation adjusts building layout, furniture and materials and edits Building, Items and Floor texture regions and UVs. A local region of the Items chair texture uses VamTastic-derived material, recorded as approximately 31,151 pixels (about 0.74% of the 2048 atlas).
